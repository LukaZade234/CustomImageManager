Welcome to ImgManager

Manage your character collection with ease. Search, customize, and organize your favorite characters.

How to use:

  • Search: Use the search bar above to find characters by Name or Series.
  • Customize: Click a character to upload custom images (Drag & Drop supported!) or change their thumbnail.
  • Save: Click the bookmark icon to add characters to your personal list.
  • Add: Missing a character? Use "Add Character" to register them in the database.
